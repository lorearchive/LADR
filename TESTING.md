# Testing LADR

Welcome! This guide outlines all the necessary information a pre-release tester may need to know before trying out and testing LADR.

## Reporting issues
First of all, thank you for checking out LADR! I am excited that you are interested in making LADR better for all.

If you find an issue, or something that doesn't seem quite right, you can create an issue in the [Issue Tracker](https://github.com/lorearchive/LADR/issues).

If there already is an IDENTICAL issue in the issue tracker, feel free to leave a comment. The more comments on identical issues, the higher the severity gets and the issue receives more priority.

You can also email me at `cirrow@proton.me`.

## The backlog

In the [LADR Backlog](https://github.com/orgs/lorearchive/projects/1/views/1) is where you'll find the next steps for LADR. Even if the issue you are reporting is on there already, you can still open an issue! This just tells me that this feature is of high priority.
